## Template assets

The uploaded template supplied its own decorative imagery and icons. Those files are
kept exactly as delivered inside `public/images/` (`hero_bg.jpg`, `img_*.jpg`,
`person_*.jpg`) and `public/fonts/` (icomoon and brand icon fonts). They are used only
where the original template used them, and they were not recoloured or replaced.

## Adding new photography

1. Choose a Pexels photo that shows East African subjects in an education setting.
2. Download at full resolution, resize the long edge to 1200 px, strip metadata and save
   as a progressive JPEG at quality 85 or above.
3. Name the file in lowercase with hyphens, for example `tutor-firstname-lastname.jpg`.
4. Add a row to the table above with the photographer and the Pexels photo URL.
